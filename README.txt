Y2K APP Verse - site files
==========================
index.html  : the whole website
assets/     : your avatar and the three app mockups

Put it online (free):
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag this whole folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a link. Rename it in Site settings.

Edit later:
- Open index.html in any text editor. Search for "var sites=" to edit apps, links and descriptions.
- To make the social preview image work, change og:image in index.html to your full URL, e.g. https://yoursite.netlify.app/assets/avatar.png
- Replace the images in assets/ with new ones using the same file names.

Icons: favicon.ico and assets/favicon-32.png / favicon-180.png are your browser tab and home-screen icons (made from your avatar).
