    ADD AN APP TO YOUR DEVICE

    📱 IPHONE / IPAD

    Use Safari
    1. Open the app in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
    3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
    4. Tap Add. The icon now sits on your home screen.

    🤖 ANDROID

    Use Chrome
    1. Open the app in Chrome.
    2. Tap the menu (three dots) at the top right.
    3. Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
    4. Tap Install or Add to confirm.

    💻 PC / MAC

    Chrome or Edge
    1. Open the app.
    2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar. Or open the menu: Chrome > Cast, save and share > Install page as app. Edge > Apps > Install this site as an app.
    3. Click Install.
    4. It opens in its own window. Pin it to your taskbar or dock. (Mac Safari: File > Add to Dock.)

    Don’t see “Install”? “Add to Home screen” always works and makes a shortcut.
